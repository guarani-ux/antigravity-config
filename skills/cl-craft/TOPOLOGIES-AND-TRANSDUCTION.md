## 2. The 4-Sentence Transduction Formula

Every proof paragraph in `/cl-draft` transforms a `STORY-*` record from `vault/career-quarry.md` into four causal sentences:

1. **Sentence 1 — Friction / Constraint**:
   Ground the real operational bottleneck, resource scarcity, or stakeholder tension before you stepped in.
2. **Sentence 2 — Judgment Call & Trade-off**:
   Reveal the non-obvious decision you made—and what conventional shortcut you deliberately refused or traded off.
3. **Sentence 3 — Measurable / Tangible Result**:
   State the exact verified outcome from `vault/career-quarry.md`.
4. **Sentence 4 — Direct Transfer to Target Mandate**:
   Bridge that proven mechanism directly into the target employer's 90-day mandate from `jd-xray.md`.

## 3. The 4 Structural Argument Topologies

### Topology A: The Direct Mandate Bullseye
- **When to Use**: Linear advancement in the same domain (for example, Specialist $\rightarrow$ Senior Specialist / Lead).
- **Psychological Goal**: Prove zero ramp-up risk; show you already operate at this scope.
- **4-Beat Structure**:
  1. *Beat 1*: Name their scaling/operational bottleneck and your immediate alignment.
  2. *Beat 2*: 4-Sentence Transduction of your primary throughput/execution story.
  3. *Beat 3*: Scope elevation—how your workflow or governance lifted cross-functional teams.
  4. *Beat 4*: First-90-days operational focus and direct close.

### Topology B: The Unfair Combination Bridge
- **When to Use**: The role sits at the collision of two silos that rarely live in the same candidate (for example, *Hands-on Broadcast Media Directing + Data-Informed Channel Strategy & Institutional Governance*).
- **Psychological Goal**: Show that hiring you solves a tension that single-discipline specialists cannot fix.
- **4-Beat Structure**:
  1. *Beat 1*: Declare the intersectional thesis (why this role requires both halves of the brain).
  2. *Beat 2*: Proof Pillar 1 — Systems, channel throughput, or strategic architecture (`STORY-CPL-001`).
  3. *Beat 3*: Proof Pillar 2 — On-the-floor directing, audience scripting, and stakeholder trust (`STORY-CPL-002` / `STORY-CPL-003`).
  4. *Beat 4*: Integrated multiplier close.

### Topology C: The Asymmetric Career Pivot
- **When to Use**: Moving across sectors (for example, Civic/Library Media Production $\rightarrow$ Tech Scale-Up B2B Content Lead or Corporate Infrastructure Communications).
- **Psychological Goal**: Prove that your origin environment was a tougher, higher-constraint proving ground for the exact mechanic they need.
- **4-Beat Structure**:
  1. *Beat 1*: Name the shared underlying mechanic across both sectors.
  2. *Beat 2*: Crucible proof story showing high-output delivery under strict constraints.
  3. *Beat 3*: Methodological translation (how multi-audience scripting and live partner adaptation solve their commercial/technical communication bottleneck).
  4. *Beat 4*: High-agency close.

### Topology D: The Institutional / Civic Merit Case
- **When to Use**: Universities (UCalgary, MRU, SAIT), municipalities (City of Calgary), public agencies, and cultural institutions operating under `Institutional Rubric Mode`.
- **Psychological Goal**: Satisfy every KSA rubric point while demonstrating public stewardship, accessibility rigor (WCAG/captions), and multi-stakeholder harmony.
- **4-Beat Structure**:
  1. *Beat 1*: Align with the institution's public charter, faculty mission, or civic mandate (referencing exact role title/requisition).
  2. *Beat 2*: Multi-stakeholder & channel operations proof story (`STORY-CPL-001` + cross-departmental coordination).
  3. *Beat 3*: Community/partner inclusion, audience adaptation, and accessibility quality assurance (`STORY-CPL-002` + `STORY-CPL-003`).
  4. *Beat 4*: Long-horizon institutional commitment close.

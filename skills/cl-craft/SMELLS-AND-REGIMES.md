## 1. The 20-Second Eye-Tracking Triage Sequence

Recruiters and hiring managers do not read cover letters linearly; they perform **3-to-5 second threat/utility triage** followed by a **15-second F-pattern scan**:

1. **Fixation 1 (0–2s) — The Upper-Left Anchor**: Eyes hit the first 1.5 lines of Paragraph 1. If the brain sees *"I am writing to express my enthusiastic interest..."*, it registers zero informational delta and bounces.
2. **Fixation 2 (2–5s) — Left-Margin Vertical Sweep**: Gaze drops down the left edge of Paragraphs 2 and 3, hunting for concrete numerals (`42 vs. 23`, `82%`), named systems, and visual breathing room.
3. **Fixation 3 (5–12s) — The Proof Lock**: When a real metric or specific operational trade-off catches the eye, the reader reads horizontally across that proof paragraph.
4. **Fixation 4 (12–18s) — The Sign-Off**: Checks professional stature and call to conversation.

## 2. Selection Regime Calibration (`Mandate` vs. `Institutional Rubric`)

| Dimension | `Private Mandate Mode` (Tech, B2B, Agencies, Scale-ups) | `Institutional Rubric Mode` (Universities, Civic, Libraries, Healthcare) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Evaluator** | Time-pressed Hiring Manager / VP | Multi-member Search Committee / HR Panel |
| **Core Question** | *"Will this person take painful work off my plate next month?"* | *"Can we award auditable rubric points for every Minimum & Desirable Qualification?"* |
| **Word Budget** | **250 – 320 words** (4 crisp paragraphs) | **400 – 550 words** (1 full page) |
| **Layer 1 Boilerplate** | Omit from letter; leave to resume | Explicitly confirm Minimum Qualifications in concise narrative clauses |
| **Auto-Detection Clues** | *"Fast-paced"*, *"Series A/B"*, *"Wear multiple hats"*, *"Scale"* | *"Requisition #"*, *"Minimum Qualifications"*, *"Search Committee"*, *"Collective Bargaining"*, *"MaPS / AUPE"* |

## 3. Forensic Taxonomy of the 12 LLM Cover Letter Smells

### Smell 1: The Sycophantic Throat-Clearing Opener (`RULE_NO_FLATTERY_OPENER`)
- **Tell**: *"I was thrilled to discover the opening at [Company]. I have long admired your unwavering commitment to..."*
- **Fix**: Open directly with the operational tension of the role and your thesis within 35 words.

### Smell 2: The Symmetric Triad Rhythm (`RULE_ASYMMETRIC_SYNTAX`)
- **Tell**: Grouping three parallel gerunds or adjectives (*"designing scalable workflows, streamlining cross-functional teams, and optimizing digital channels"*).
- **Fix**: Use 1 concrete focal action with deep context or 2 contrasting poles.

### Smell 3: Adjective-Adverb Clustering (`RULE_ZERO_EMPTY_INTENSIFIERS`)
- **Tell**: Stacking decorative modifiers (*deeply passionate, seamlessly orchestrating, pivotal, transformative, spearheaded, synergy, testament*).
- **Fix**: Delete every intensifier; let concrete nouns and verified numbers carry weight.

### Smell 4: Chronological Resume Necromancy (`RULE_NON_CHRONOLOGICAL_PROOF`)
- **Tell**: *"My career began in 2021 at X... Throughout my career, I have..."*
- **Fix**: Organize strictly by causal argument, leading with the single strongest proof story for their #1 pain.

### Smell 5: Presumptive Self-Evaluation (`RULE_NO_SELF_EVALUATION`)
- **Tell**: *"My unique blend of skills makes me the ideal candidate for..."*
- **Fix**: Present raw empirical evidence and let the reader conclude you are the fit.

### Smell 6: Contextless Vague Metrics (`RULE_GROUNDED_CAUSAL_METRICS`)
- **Tell**: *"Boosted efficiency by 40% through strategic content planning."*
- **Fix**: State the baseline, final number, and physical mechanism (*"Scaled annual output from 23 published videos in 2024 to 42 in 2025 by restructuring shoots around modular two-part batching"*).

### Smell 7: Dangling Participial Openers (`RULE_ACTIVE_DECLARATIVE_SYNTAX`)
- **Tell**: *"Having successfully led multi-camera productions for three years, I understand..."* / *"Leveraging my background in..."*
- **Fix**: Lead with the active subject and verb (*"At Calgary Public Library, I restructured..."*).

### Smell 8: Melodramatic Metaphor Inflation (`RULE_LITERAL_ENTERPRISE_GROUNDING`)
- **Tell**: *Tapestry, beacon, journey, symphony, catalyst, cornerstone.*
- **Fix**: Use plain, exact craft and operational nouns.

### Smell 9: Homogenized Paragraph Blocks (`RULE_DYNAMIC_TYPOGRAPHY`)
- **Tell**: Four identical 5-sentence blocks of uniform visual thickness.
- **Fix**: Vary paragraph geometry (2–3 line hook, 4–5 line proof pillars, 2-line close).

### Smell 10: Self-Centered Agency Inversion (`RULE_EMPLOYER_MANDATE_CENTRIC`)
- **Tell**: *"This role would be a great next step for my career growth and allow me to explore my passion for..."*
- **Fix**: Frame 85% of sentences around how your judgment solves *their* bottleneck.

### Smell 11: Abstract Buzzword Layering (`RULE_CONCRETE_NOUN_ANCHOR`)
- **Tell**: *"I drive cross-functional alignment to operationalize strategic content paradigms."*
- **Fix**: Every sentence must name at least one tangible artifact (a specific series, tool, stakeholder group, metric, or deliverable).

### Smell 12: Obsequious Diplomatic Closing (`RULE_CONFIDENT_DIRECT_CLOSE`)
- **Tell**: *"Should you find my qualifications aligned, I would be deeply humbled and grateful..."*
- **Fix**: Close as a peer proposing a direct working conversation.

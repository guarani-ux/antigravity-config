Organization: Calgary Public Library  |  Platform: YouTube (Public Release)  |  Audience: Parents & caregivers of children ages 0–5 (Walk-in state: tired in winter, assuming libraries are either quiet book-rooms or that ELCs are just indoor playgrounds)  |  Production Mode: Mode B — Pre-Shoot Documentary / SME Hybrid (Kristen Duke VO + On-Camera + Caregiver Bench Snippets)

Story Spine & Causal Premise

Leading Word: "The Look Back." A two-year-old only tackles hard puzzles and builds early reading confidence on the floor when they feel safe—and they know they're safe because every time something clicks, they look back at the bench around the room where their parent finally has a place to exhale. Over ten years and 13 locations since Fish Creek opened in 2016, CPL didn't just build play areas; they built a living room around the whole family.

Director's Field Interview Guide

Kristen Duke (Early Learning Specialist):
  • The Surface vs. Reality Prompt: "When a parent walks in here out of the cold for the first time, what does it look like to them—and what are you actually watching happen on this floor?"
  • The Single-Moment Prompt (Anecdote): "Point to one piece in this room—a puzzle, a ramp, a nook. Walk me through what happens when a two-year-old gets stuck on it and figures it out."
  • The Physical Bridge (The Look Back): "What is the very first thing a toddler does the second they figure something out—who do they look for, and why does that bench around the edge matter so much?"
  • The 10-Year Human Arc: "A kid who crawled into Fish Creek in 2016 is thirteen years old now. What changes when you grow up thinking of the library as your living room?"
On-Site Parents & Caregivers (Candid Bench Grabs — Seated Eye-Level):
  • The Winter Reality: "It's minus twenty-five in January and you're home alone with a toddler. What does walking through those doors feel like?"
  • The Shared Exhale: "What happens the moment you sit down on this bench next to another parent who's in the exact same boat?"
